Hairline density, no surgery.

Female hair loss often shows up as a widening part, diffuse thinning or more visible scalp. Density enhancement reduces scalp contrast beneath existing hair for a fuller look.

Consultation15–30 minutes. We assess your hair loss pattern and design your plan.
First session1–3 hours depending on the area. Ultra-fine pigment shading is applied.
2-week touch-upRequired. It perfects the result and builds final density.
  • Soft, natural-looking results
  • Blends with your existing hair
  • No harsh shaved-head look
  • Density builds gradually over multiple sessions

How many sessions will I need?

Most paramedical and PMU procedures take 1 to 3 sessions, spaced 4–6 weeks apart, for the best depth and longevity.

Is there any downtime?

Downtime is minimal. Mild redness or swelling can last 24–48 hours, and you’ll get full aftercare instructions.
