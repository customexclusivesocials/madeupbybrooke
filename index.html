Our Specialized Treatments

Precision cosmetic and paramedical tattooing tailored to your individual skin needs.

Scalp Micropigmentation (SMP)

Creates the look of fuller, denser hair or a clean, defined shaved hairline by placing micro-impressions into the scalp. Ideal for thinning hair, receding hairlines, bald spots, or scalp scarring.

Hairline Density Enhancement

Ultra-fine pigment shading reduces scalp contrast beneath existing hair for the appearance of fuller density. A soft, natural option for women with a widening part or diffuse thinning.

Lip Blush

Scar Camouflage

Eyebrow PMU Correction

Hairline Density Enhancement

A natural-looking cosmetic option for thinning hair — no surgery, no shaved-head look.

What to Expect

  • Consultation 15–30 mins We assess your hair loss pattern and design a customized density enhancement plan.
  • First Session 1–3 hours, depending on the area Ultra-fine pigment shading is applied to reduce scalp contrast and create a natural density effect.
  • 2-Week Touch-Up Required A required touch-up session perfects the result and builds the final density.

Female Hair Loss & Hair Density Enhancement

Female hair loss affects millions of women and often appears as a widening part, diffuse thinning, or increased scalp visibility.

Hair density implantation helps create the appearance of fuller hair by reducing scalp contrast beneath existing hair, providing a natural-looking cosmetic enhancement without surgery.

  • Softer, more natural-looking results
  • Ideal for thinning areas and sparse hairlines
  • No harsh shaved-head appearance
  • Creates the illusion of fuller density
  • Blends seamlessly with existing hair
  • Build density gradually over multiple sessions

Frequently Asked Questions

How many sessions will I need?

Most paramedical and PMU procedures require 2 to 3 sessions spaced 4-6 weeks apart to achieve optimal depth and longevity.

Is there any downtime?

Downtime is minimal. Mild redness or swelling may occur for 24-48 hours. Comprehensive aftercare instructions will be provided.
