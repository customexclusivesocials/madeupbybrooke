What I Offer

Six treatments, one restorative goal.

Every service below is personalized in an initial consultation — scar tissue, skin tone, and hair pattern are never treated with a one-size template.

01 — Paramedical

Scar Camouflage Tattooing

Custom-matched, colour pigment tattooed into scar tissue to blend it seamlessly into your surrounding skin tone — for surgical, trauma, and discolouration scarring.

Good candidates
  • Red, purple, blue, or white scarring
  • Tummy tucks, C-sections, breast surgery
  • Facelifts & previous tattoo/PMU camouflage
02 — Paramedical

Inkless Scar Revision (ISR)

A pigment-free, medical-grade serum worked into the skin with a specialized needling technique — targeting texture, not colour, to soften and rebuild scar tissue.

Good candidates
  • Raised, indented, or uneven-texture scars
  • Stretch marks of most skin tones
  • Clients who want repair over concealment
03 — Permanent Makeup

Lip Blushing & Dark Lip Neutralization

Semi-permanent lip tattooing for fuller, more defined colour that lasts up to three years — plus a specialized neutralizing technique for darker or cool-toned lips.

Good candidates
  • Anyone wanting a "your lips, but better" look
  • Melanin-rich or cool/darker lip tones
  • Clients wanting to retire daily lip liner
04 — Permanent Makeup

PMU Eyebrow Colour Correction

Corrective work for previous brow tattoos that have faded, shifted colour, or healed unevenly — restoring a soft, balanced, natural pigment.

Good candidates
  • Faded, ashy, or discoloured old PMU brows
  • Uneven healing from a previous artist
  • Clients ready for a natural colour reset
05 — Paramedical

Scalp Micropigmentation (SMP)

Tiny impressions of pigment placed into the scalp to create the illusion of fuller, denser hair or a clean, defined shaved hairline — for men and women.

Good candidates
  • Male & female pattern baldness, thinning hair
  • Alopecia, full or partial coverage
  • Hair transplant (FUE/FUT) & trauma scarring
06 — Paramedical

Micro Feathering (Hairline & Part Density)

A softer, feathered pigment technique built for women's hairlines and thinning part lines — filling visible gaps without the flat, shaved look of full SMP.

Good candidates
  • Receding or thinning hairlines
  • Widening centre parts
  • Post-transplant density along the hairline

The Process

Your treatment journey.

Every restorative and cosmetic procedure follows the same careful sequence, from your first phone call to your final healed result.

01

Complimentary Consultation

Book a free consultation, in person or by phone, to discuss your goals and the best treatment path. A digital mock-up can be arranged for a clearer picture of your healed result.

02

Booking & Deposit

A deposit may be required to secure your first appointment. It's always applied toward the full balance of your treatment — nothing is lost.

03

Session One — The Foundation Treatment

We map the treatment area and custom-blend your pigment or medical-grade serum to match your exact skin tone before any tattooing begins.

04

Healing Window

Most treatments need several weeks to fully settle as your body's natural healing response takes over — aftercare guidance is provided at every session.

05

Follow-Up & Perfecting Sessions

Additional sessions build on the last, refining colour, density, or texture until your result is complete. Exact session counts vary by treatment — see below.

Set Expectations

Expected sessions, by treatment.

Because paramedical and cosmetic tattooing relies on your body's own healing response, results build gradually. Here's the general clinical timeline.

Scalp Micropigmentation

3–4 sessions

Built through micro-layers of pigment to create the 3D illusion of natural hair density and depth.

Lip Blushing

2 sessions

A foundation session and a perfecting session. Dark lip neutralization may require 3 or more sessions.

Scar Camouflage

2–4 sessions

Scar tissue absorbs pigment differently than healthy skin, requiring careful, gradual layering for an undetectable blend.

Inkless Scar Revision

3–6 sessions

Relies on stimulating your body's own collagen production, which requires multiple treatments spaced out over time.

Micro Feathering

2–3 sessions

Density is built gradually along the hairline or part to keep the result soft, natural, and undetectable.

PMU Brow Colour Correction

2–3 sessions

Exact count depends on the pigment and technique used in your original brow tattoo.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions.

What should I expect at my first appointment?+
A 15–30 minute in-clinic consultation is recommended before your first treatment. This allows us to assess the area, discuss your goals, determine the best approach, and walk through personalized aftercare — plus a clear breakdown of your treatment cost before we begin.
How much does treatment cost?+
Because every permanent makeup and paramedical treatment is highly personalized, pricing is provided individually rather than as one standard rate. You'll receive a full breakdown during your consultation, before any deposit is taken.
Is scar or paramedical tattooing painful?+
A topical numbing agent is used throughout every treatment, so most clients describe the sensation as mild pressure or vibration rather than pain. Sensitivity varies by treatment area and by client.
How long do results last?+
Results are long-lasting but not permanent — most treatments hold their appearance for one to three years depending on the technique, your skin, sun exposure, and skincare routine, with simple top-up sessions to refresh colour or density over time.
Who is not a good candidate?+
Clients who are pregnant or breastfeeding, have contagious skin conditions or active infections in the treatment area, raised moles or warts on the site, a history of skin cancer or radiation therapy within the last year, or broken/compromised skin should hold off until cleared — this will always be discussed at consultation.
