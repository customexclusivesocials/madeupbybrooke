The Process

Your journey to restored skin.

Restorative tattooing requires patience, precision, and personalized care. Here is what to expect from start to finish.

01

Complimentary Consultation

We review your skin history, scar age, medical readiness, and treatment goals. Together, we establish a realistic session plan and test pigment shades if applicable.

02

Custom Pigment Mapping & Prep

Before any tattooing begins, pigment shades are custom-blended under natural daylight, and skin mapping is approved by you down to the millimeter.

03

Precision Session

Using specialized micro-needles and medical-grade pigments, your treatment is applied in controlled layers to safeguard skin integrity and comfort.

04

Aftercare & Layered Refinement

You’ll receive a clinical aftercare kit and precise instructions. Healing takes 4–6 weeks per session, allowing us to evaluate pigment settling before optional touch-ups.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is paramedical scar camouflage painful?

Most clients experience minimal discomfort. Topical numbing agents are applied prior to and during the procedure to ensure you remain comfortable throughout your session.

How old must a scar be before it can be tattooed?

Scars must be fully healed, white or pale in color, and at least 12–18 months old before pigment or inkless revision can safely begin. Red, pink, or hyperpigmented scars cannot be tattooed directly.

How many sessions will I need for optimal results?

Paramedical camouflage is a layered process. Most scar and stretch mark treatments require 2 to 3 sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart to build natural coverage safely.

How long do permanent makeup and camouflage pigments last?

Paramedical camouflage pigments typically last 3 to 5 years (or longer depending on sun exposure and skin type). Cosmetic PMU like lip blushing and brows generally last 2 to 3 years before a color refresh is desired.
