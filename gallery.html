01 — Paramedical Tattooing

Scar Camouflage Tattooing

Custom-matched pigment tattooed directly into scar tissue, blending it seamlessly into your surrounding skin tone — suitable for surgical, trauma, and discolouration scarring of nearly every kind.

Good Candidates
  • Hypopigmented (white) or pale scarring
  • Tummy tucks, C-sections, breast surgery
  • Facelifts, trauma scars & skin graft borders
Typically 2–4 sessions
02 — Paramedical Revision

Inkless Scar Revision (ISR)

A specialized serum-based rotary technique that stimulates your body's natural cellular renewal, rebuilding collagen, elastin, and melanin to improve scar texture and depth without pigment.

Good Candidates
  • Stretch marks (striae alba & rubra)
  • Textured, raised, or tethered surgical scars
  • Hyperpigmented or uneven scar tissue
Typically 1–3 sessions
03 — Permanent Makeup

Lip Blushing & Neutralization

Subtle, customized sheer pigment implantation to restore symmetry, define lip vermilion borders, correct cool undertones, and deliver a healthy natural wash of colour.

Good Candidates
  • Loss of natural lip border and shape
  • Cool, pale, or uneven natural lip tones
  • Melanin-rich lip neutralization needs
Typically 1–2 sessions
04 — Corrective PMU

Eyebrow PMU & Colour Correction

Advanced corrective tattooing using warm or neutralizing target modifiers to adjust old faded PMU (red, blue, grey, or purple brows) back to natural, flattering tones alongside fresh structural design.

Good Candidates
  • Brows shifted to blue, grey, pink, or orange
  • Asymmetry from previous permanent makeup
  • Clients wanting soft ombre/powder restoration
Typically 2–3 sessions
05 — Density & Restoration

Scalp Micropigmentation (SMP)

Precision micro-deposit follicular impressions replicated across the scalp to camouflage hair thinning, restore receding hairlines, and conceal FUE/FUT hair transplant surgical scars.

Good Candidates
  • Diffuse hair thinning across crown or part line
  • Receding hairlines & male/female pattern hair loss
  • FUT/FUE hair transplant strip or punch scars
Typically 2–4 sessions
06 — Hairline Detailing

Hair Micro Feathering

Ultra-fine, directional micro-stroke tattooing tailored specifically to soften hairline perimeters, temples, and frontal growth patterns for an undetectable, soft natural frame.

Good Candidates
  • Sparse temple zones & traction alopecia edges
  • Clients seeking natural soft wisps along hairline
  • Post-surgical temple pull-back or scarring
Typically 1–2 sessions
