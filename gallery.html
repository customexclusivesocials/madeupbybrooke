02 — Paramedical Tattooing

Inkless Scar Revision (ISR)

A pigment-free, medical-grade serum worked into the skin with a specialized needling technique. No colour is added — this treatment targets texture, stimulating your body's own collagen to soften and rebuild scar tissue.

Good Candidates
  • Raised, indented, or uneven-texture scars
  • Stretch marks across most skin tones
  • Clients who want repair over concealment
Typically 3–6 sessions
Texture Detail — Before
MCA Needling Technique

Expect mild redness immediately after treatment that softens over several weeks — this is a restorative process, not an overnight fix.

05 — Paramedical Tattooing

Scalp Micropigmentation (SMP)

Tiny impressions of pigment placed into the scalp to create the illusion of fuller, denser hair or a clean, defined shaved hairline — built through micro-layers for men and women alike.

Good Candidates
  • Male & female pattern baldness, thinning hair
  • Alopecia — full or partial coverage
  • Hair transplant (FUE/FUT) & trauma scarring
Typically 3–4 sessions
